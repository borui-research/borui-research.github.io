About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The Pennsylvania State University, advised by Prof. Jun Xu. My research focuses on the mechanical safety of next-generation batteries, electro-chemo-mechanical coupling, multiphysics modeling, and the experimental mechanics of energy storage systems.

Before joining Penn State, I was a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Delaware. I received my M.S. in Solid Mechanics from Shanghai University, advised by Prof. Bo Lu, and my B.S. in Civil Engineering from Shanghai Normal University.
