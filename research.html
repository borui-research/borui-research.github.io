Research Interests

Current directions

Electro-Chemo-Mechanical Coupling and Multiphysics Modeling

Coupled behaviors involving stack pressure, lithium plating, aging, internal short circuits, and electrode degradation, studied using COMSOL, Abaqus, MATLAB, and HPC-based models.

Battery Safety and Thermo-Mechanical Behavior

Mechanical abuse, thermal runaway, separator failure, and electrolyte-coupled temperature-dependent behavior of lithium-ion and sodium-ion battery components.
